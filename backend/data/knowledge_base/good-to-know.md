# Good to Know Before You Order

## Shipping charges
All prices are exclusive of shipping charges, which are shared when you order.

## Truly handmade
Every piece is poured and finished by hand, so no two products will look exactly the same. Small variations in colour, shape and finish are part of their charm.

## Product images
Product images are for representation only. The piece you receive may differ slightly.

## Colours
Colours may look slightly different on your screen due to lighting and display settings.

## Prices
Prices are valid till the date mentioned in the catalogue and may change without prior notice.

## Individual scents
Individual scent names are not listed in the catalogue. Ask Liora on WhatsApp at 85913 53636 which scents are available.

## Availability
Scents, colours and designs are subject to availability.

## Custom and bulk order notice
Personalised and bulk orders need advance notice. Details are confirmed on WhatsApp before we begin.

## Damage in transit
If your order arrives damaged, message us within 24 hours with photos or an unboxing video.
