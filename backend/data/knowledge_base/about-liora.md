# About Liora By Srijana

## The brand
Liora By Srijana makes candles that are hand-poured, scented and soulful. The brand's line is "Scents that make life brighter".

## Who makes the candles
Srijana pours every Liora candle by hand in small batches, made slowly, finished with care, and meant to make everyday moments a little brighter. Every candle you bring home supports a small dream and a lot of love.

## What Liora makes
The range covers scented jar candles, wax sachets, wax melts, tea lights, urlis, taper candles, shaped candles, roses and peonies, candle bouquets, Diwali hampers, and customised gifting.

## What Liora stands for
Premium quality raw material, thoughtfully made, inspired by nature, and a more conscious choice.
