# Candle Care

## Before lighting
Trim the wick to 5 mm for a cleaner, longer burn.

## While burning
Let the wax melt evenly. Avoid burning a candle for longer than 3–4 hours at a time.

## Keep away from
Keep candles away from drafts, pets, children and flammable materials. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

## After use
Let the candle cool, then keep the lid on to preserve the fragrance.

## Give it a second life
Once the scent fades, clean the jar and reuse it, or break the leftover wax into pieces and use them as wax melts. The fragrance still remains, ready to fill your space with warmth.

## Storing candles
Wax can soften in heat. Store candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
